Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 18, 2026

1. Scope

This privacy policy describes how the clink SMS program ("the Program"), operated by Jarrid Baldwin ("Operator") as a personal sole-proprietor service, handles information related to its single authorized user. The Program is a private, self-use AI assistant. The Operator and the sole recipient of all messages are the same person.

2. Information Collected

  • Mobile phone number of the Operator (used for SMS delivery).
  • Inbound text content from the Operator (used to generate responses).
  • Message delivery metadata provided by Twilio and the underlying carrier.

3. How Information Is Used

Collected information is used solely to operate the personal assistant — delivering reminders, calendar notifications, email summaries, stock alerts, and responses to inbound commands. No marketing or promotional use.

4. Sharing

The Operator does not sell, rent, or share any information with third parties for marketing purposes. SMS message content and phone numbers are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Messages are transmitted through Twilio Inc. and U.S. mobile carriers solely to deliver the SMS; those providers handle the data under their own privacy policies.

5. Retention

Message logs are retained only as long as necessary to operate the service and are deleted when no longer needed.

6. Security

Reasonable technical measures are taken to protect data in transit and at rest, including API-key-based authentication and encrypted storage for credentials.

7. Your Rights

As the sole user, the Operator may delete any stored data at any time. Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe and halt future texts.
